SMB LMS Guide 2026

For teams under 100 learners, the published-price LMS market is small, the discount is real, and the time to launch is short.

The SMB shortlist

For teams under 100 learners: TalentLMS publishes tier prices ($0 Free, $119 Core to 40, $229 Grow to 70, $449 Pro to 100). 360Learning publishes a per-user rate ($8/user/month, capped at 100). iSpring offers a 30-day full-feature trial but does not publish a rate. Moodle LMS Core is free under GPL if you self-host. Litmos Foundation serves up to 250 learners but is quote-only. Everything else in the market expects you to be bigger.

Why SMBs pay less

Three structural reasons

  • Published rates compete. TalentLMS and 360Learning compete head-to-head on transparent rates because their target buyer compares prices before talking to sales. Enterprise vendors compete on RFP and bundling.
  • Implementation services are minimal. SMB LMS is configured by an admin in an afternoon, not delivered by a consultant over six months. That removes one of the largest enterprise cost lines.
  • The product is the deal. SMB vendors sell self-serve. Sales cost per dollar of contract is dramatically lower, and that shows up in the rate card.

A worked example

Acme SaaS Co., 65 learners, growing 30 percent year on year

Illustrative example, not a real company. Numbers chosen to demonstrate methodology.

Acme has 65 learners today and expects 85 in twelve months. Published-price options:

  • TalentLMS Grow today ($229/mo, up to 70 users), switching to Pro ($449/mo, up to 100) once Acme passes 70. Year-one total: roughly six months at Grow and six months at Pro, $4,068/year. Year two: Pro all twelve months, $5,388/year.
  • 360Learning Team at $8/user/month: 65 users today is $520/mo, $6,240/year. 85 users next year is $680/mo, $8,160/year. The per-user model tracks growth smoothly but costs more in absolute dollars at this learner count.
  • The crossover: TalentLMS Pro is cheaper than 360Learning Team for any learner count above about 56 users, because TalentLMS Pro is a flat $449 and 360Learning scales linearly.
When to look outside the published-price set

Signals you have outgrown SMB

  • You crossed 100 learners or expect to within twelve months.
  • You need multi-portal architecture (separate employee, partner, customer learning).
  • You need HRIS, CRM, or video-engagement integrations beyond standard SSO.
  • Your compliance training needs audit trails the published-price set does not document.
