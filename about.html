About lmscost.com

Independent LMS pricing reference. Written and maintained by Oliver Wakefield-Smith, founder of Digital Signet.

What this site is

A verifiable pricing reference for the LMS category

Every dollar figure on this site traces back to a vendor pricing page that we fetched on the verification date stamped on the source row. Quote-only vendors carry no inferred rates. The intent is a buyer-side reference that does not invent numbers when vendors decline to publish them.

What this site is not

No sponsorship, no affiliate links, no email capture

  • No vendor pays for placement. No vendor is excluded for refusing to sponsor.
  • No affiliate links. External links are vendor pricing or product pages.
  • No email capture. No lead-gen forms. No newsletter signup.
  • No webinar funnels, no gated PDFs, no chatbots.
Who maintains it

Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Digital Signet

Digital Signet maintains a small portfolio of independent reference sites in pricing-opaque software categories. lmscost.com is one of those references. If you find an error, the right thing to do is email a correction; the source-page URL and the corrected number are the only things we need.

Editorial principles

The four rules we apply to every page

  1. Every number traces to a vendor pricing page URL, fetched on a stated date.
  2. Quote-only vendors get the same depth as published-price vendors, but no inferred rate.
  3. Worked examples use fictional company names labelled illustrative.
  4. Each page carries a Last verified stamp with the honest verification date.
How to use this site

Three buyer journeys

  • Cost calculator: plug in your learner count and see the published rates compared.
  • Buyer guides: SMB, mid-market, enterprise, sales enablement, customer education, partner training.
  • Topic essays: RFP template, hidden costs, AI features, the quote-only trap.
