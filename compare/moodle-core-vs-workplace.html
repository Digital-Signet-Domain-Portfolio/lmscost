Moodle Core vs Moodle Workplace

Two different Moodle products, often confused as one. Core is open source, free under GPL. Workplace is licensed enterprise software sold through partners.

The single sentence

Moodle LMS Core is free under GPL and you can self-host it forever. Moodle Workplace is a separate proprietary product sold only through Moodle Premium Certified Partners, with no public rate. If somebody told you “Moodle is free for enterprise”, they conflated the two.

The real cost of Core

Free is not zero

Moodle Core’s licence cost is $0, but the operating cost is not. A realistic self-hosted Moodle Core deployment carries the following cost lines:

  • Hosting: VPS or managed PHP hosting, with database and storage.
  • Engineering time: install, theme, configure, integrate (SSO, HRIS), maintain, upgrade twice yearly.
  • Plugin compatibility: every minor upgrade can break plugins. Test before promoting.
  • Accessibility: WCAG compliance auditing on themes and custom code.
  • Support: in-house or contracted to a Moodle partner.

That cost is real and recurring. It is not always cheaper than a SaaS LMS at small scale. For an honest comparison, build a full TCO model that includes engineer time at the same loaded rate you use for any other infra-vs-SaaS decision.

When Workplace earns its premium

Features Core does not match

  • Multi-tenant organisational hierarchies (parent + child companies).
  • Workflow approvals (manager-approved learning plans).
  • Programs and learning plans tied to job role and team.
  • Automated certifications with expiry and recertification cycles.
  • Native reporting tuned to corporate L&D rather than academic outcomes.
