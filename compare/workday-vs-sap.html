Workday Learning vs SAP SuccessFactors Learning

Two enterprise HCM suites whose learning modules are sold inside the broader HCM contract. Neither vendor publishes a standalone learning rate.

The structural truth

The choice between Workday Learning and SAP SuccessFactors Learning is rarely an LMS-only decision. It tracks the underlying HCM decision: if you are a Workday HCM shop, you take Workday Learning; if you are a SAP SuccessFactors HCM shop, you take SAP SuccessFactors Learning. Cross-buying (Workday Learning over SAP HCM, or vice versa) is rare and not a published procurement path.

Why pricing is invisible

The HCM-bundle dynamic

  • HCM suites are sold as multi-module contracts. Publishing a per-module rate would invite competitors to point-attack each module, which is the opposite of the bundle’s commercial intent.
  • Both vendors compete primarily for HCM share. Learning is a retention lever and an expansion path, not a customer acquisition lever.
  • The standalone learning pricing URL on Workday returned 404 at our last fetch. The SAP equivalent returned 403. Both behaviours are consistent with deliberate non-disclosure.
When neither fits

Alternatives outside the HCM-bundle market

  • If you are not on Workday or SuccessFactors HCM, the standalone LMS market (Cornerstone, Docebo, LearnUpon, Absorb, Litmos) is your natural set.
  • If you only need learning and want published pricing, look at the published-price set: TalentLMS, 360Learning.
  • If you want open-source, Moodle LMS Core remains free and self-hostable; Moodle Workplace is partner-channel only.
