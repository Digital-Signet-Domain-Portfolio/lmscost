TalentLMS vs 360Learning

The only two vendors in our twelve-vendor set that publish per-user or tier pricing. TalentLMS is tiered; 360Learning is per-user. The crossover math is straightforward.

Cost rule of thumb

TalentLMS wins on absolute monthly cost at every published learner count from 5 to 100, because its tier price is a flat fee per band rather than a per-user multiple. The trade-off is that TalentLMS prices step up at 40, 70, and 100 users; 360Learning’s per-user model is linear. Above 100 users, both vendors are quote-only.

Beyond price

Where each vendor genuinely differs

  • TalentLMS is the fastest time-to-live in the published-price set. Sign up, configure, launch within a day. Strong default UX, light on the admin learning curve. Free plan (5 users, 10 courses) lets you actually test it.
  • 360Learning markets collaborative authoring as its differentiator. Course authoring is built around subject-matter experts inside the team contributing, rather than an L&D specialist building everything. If that workflow matches your culture, 360Learning is the natural pick.
  • Pricing transparency is roughly equal. Both vendors publish rates clearly. Both have quote-only plans above 100 users where the conversation shifts.
  • Free trial vs free plan: TalentLMS’s free plan is permanent (capped); 360Learning offers a free trial of unspecified length.
When to choose TalentLMS

Buyer profile

  • Small or mid-size team with stable user count near a tier ceiling (40, 70, or 100).
  • You want the lowest monthly fee at your learner count.
  • You need to launch this week, not next month.
  • You want a permanent free plan to validate before paying.
When to choose 360Learning

Buyer profile

  • Collaborative authoring (subject-matter-expert-led) is the workflow you want to instil.
  • Your learner count grows by 1s and 2s rather than in step changes; per-user billing tracks that smoothly.
  • You expect to cross 100 users and want the upgrade path into 360Learning Business with combined registered + monthly-active billing.
