LMS Cost Calculator

Slider-driven estimates across twelve LMS vendors. Returns a published number for the two vendors that publish one. Flags the rest as Quote Only with no inferred rate.

What the calculator does, and does not do

Multiplies learner count by published per-user or tier prices for 360Learning and TalentLMS, and returns Quote Only for the other ten vendors. It does not estimate implementation, content, integration, or training services. It does not infer a number from G2 user-submitted prices.

What to do with the output

Use it as the anchor for a vendor RFP

  1. For SMB deployments up to 100 users, the calculator gives you two published-price comparators. Use them as the floor in any negotiation.
  2. For mid-market deployments above 100 users, the calculator flags the entire field as quote-only. Plan a 3-to-5 vendor RFP with a 60-to-90 day cycle.
  3. When you receive a quote, ask the vendor to break out: per-user rate, minimum commitment, implementation fee, content library cost, SSO/HRIS integration, and renewal-year price escalator. Each is a separate negotiation lever.
  4. Cross-check against the methodology page for what we know about each vendor’s pricing model, and the sources page for the source URLs.
