Methodology

Every dollar figure on this site traces back to a vendor pricing page that we fetched on a stated date. Quote-only vendors carry no inferred rates.

Verification method

How each number gets confirmed

Each vendor pricing URL fetched via WebFetch on the verification date. Quote-only vendors flagged where the page does not publish dollar amounts. No inferred or extrapolated rates.

Last verified: 2026-06-17. Verified by: Digital Signet research, WebFetch direct retrieval.

The calculator formula

What the calculator does and does not do

For 360Learning Team plan: monthly cost = users x $8, capped at 100 users.
For TalentLMS: pick the smallest published tier whose user-cap covers your learner count (Free 5 / Core 40 / Grow 70 / Pro 100). Above 100, route to Enterprise quote.
For all other vendors: return Quote Only with no inferred number.

The calculator does not estimate implementation fees, content libraries, integrations, AI uplift, portal upcharges, training, or renewal escalation. Those lines are listed in the hidden-costs breakdown.
